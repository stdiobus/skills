# Scientific correspondences and their limits

Verified against public primary publications or official scientific documentation on 2026-10-07. The correspondence to the supplied research history is our interpretation; the sources do not study or endorse that history. No single established name for the entire composite workflow was established by this search. That is a bounded search result, not proof that no such name exists.

| Observed operation | Appropriate name | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Inspect distributions, plots, unusual groups, and transformations before proposing a relation | Exploratory data analysis (EDA) | NIST describes graphical investigation to discover structure and anomalies. A detected pattern motivates a hypothesis; it is not automatic confirmation. [NIST, What is EDA?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section1/eda11.htm) |
| Start from an observed result and propose an explanation that could account for it | Abduction / abductive hypothesis formation | Peirce distinguishes abduction, deduction, and induction in his Harvard lectures. The source supports this logical category, not the correctness of an explanation inferred from a particular chart. [Peirce Edition Project, Harvard lectures, 1903](https://peirce.sitehost.iu.edu/ep/ep2/headers/ep2headx.htm) |
| Form alternatives and design experiments whose outcomes eliminate alternatives | Strong inference | Platt's proposed method uses competing hypotheses, discriminating experiments, and iteration. Running many parameter variants alone is insufficient to claim full adherence. [Platt, 1964, Strong Inference, Science 146:347–353](https://worthylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/platt1964.pdf), DOI: 10.1126/science.146.3642.347. |
| Separate discovery on inspected results from testing a prior prediction | Exploratory versus confirmatory research; prospective specification | Nosek et al. explain the distinction between generating and testing hypotheses. A local pre-run protocol supports that separation; call it formal preregistration only when it is actually registered prospectively. [Nosek et al., 2018, The preregistration revolution](https://psychologicalsciences.unimelb.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/2888098/The-preregistration-revolution.pdf), DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1708274114. |
| Infer unknown inputs or parameters from observations and a forward model | Inverse problem | Tarantola treats inference of model parameters from data and the information relating them. Use this name only when the forward relation and unknowns are specified; do not infer that any inverse is unique, cheap, or computable from an empirical output region. [Tarantola, 2005, The General Discrete Inverse Problem](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1.9780898717921.ch1). |
| Remove values inconsistent with explicit relations over variables | Constraint satisfaction / consistency enforcement | Mackworth formalizes variables, domains, predicates, and consistency algorithms. A heuristic sample filter is not automatically a sound constraint propagator. [Mackworth, 1977, Consistency in Networks of Relations](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~mack/Publications/AI77.pdf), DOI: 10.1016/0004-3702(77)90007-8. |
| Protect evaluation from target-related and unavailable information | Leakage control | Kapoor and Narayanan analyze leakage as a failure mode of ML-based science. Inspect derived features and the real prediction scenario, not just the declared split. [Kapoor & Narayanan, Leakage and the Reproducibility Crisis in ML-based Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07048), published in Patterns, 2023, DOI: 10.1016/j.patter.2023.100804. |
| Select among repeatedly tested configurations without overstating final performance | Control of model-selection bias | Cawley and Talbot show that optimizing an evaluation criterion can itself overfit and bias performance estimates. A ratio such as 80/20 is not sufficient protection. [Cawley & Talbot, 2010, JMLR 11:2079–2107](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html). |
| Organize alternative solution branches and evaluate continuation/backtracking | Tree of Thoughts (ToT), when the actual procedure warrants the label | Yao et al. propose an LLM inference framework exploring alternative intermediate steps. Its reported results concern their tasks; they do not certify an empirical R&D discovery. [Yao et al., 2023, Tree of Thoughts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601). |
| Calculate expected filter passage under an explicitly stated independent-trial null | Binomial model | NIST gives the probability model and its assumptions. It can be a control calculation, not proof that the real process follows those assumptions. [NIST, Binomial Distribution](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda366i.htm). |

## How to use “backward” accurately

First say which operation is intended. Inferring an explanation, solving for inputs, and eliminating candidates are different operations. Exploring records outside an empirical region has no special universally established method name demonstrated here; describe it literally as a contrastive investigation of inside/outside records. Do not present that descriptive phrase as a named standard.

“Proof by contradiction” is appropriate only for a deductive argument where an assumption leads to a contradiction under stated premises. Testing a complement, searching for a counterexample, or reversing input/output direction alone is not that proof.

If no source matches the actual operation, keep a descriptive operational name: inputs, transformation, criterion, output, verification. Do not invent an authoritative label. Further literature may refine the naming, but naming must not block a well-specified experiment.
